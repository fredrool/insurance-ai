# ABC Assurances Claims Procedure (Form C-200)

Form C-200 describes how fictional policyholders report and resolve claims with ABC Assurances. It applies to **Form H-100** (home) and **Form A-300** (auto) unless a policy section states otherwise. This document is for educational RAG training only and has no legal effect.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1 Form C-200 standardizes reporting, evidence collection, investigation, decision, payment, and appeals for property and automobile claims.

1.2 **Catastrophe protocol CP-9** may supersede timelines when a provincial catastrophe is declared by ABC Assurances' fictional Cat Desk.

1.3 **Privacy.** Personal information is handled under fictional Privacy Standard ABC-PRIV-3 for demo datasets.

1.4 **Emergency.** For injury or ongoing danger, insureds must contact emergency services before calling ABC Assurances.

## 2. Reporting a Claim

2.1 **How to report.** Use the fictional portal claims.abc-assurances.edu, phone 1-800-ABC-DEMO, or in-app messaging when enrolled in ABC Digital (fictional).

2.2 **Timelines.**

2.2.1 **Property (H-100):** notify within 7 days of discovery; water losses require initial notice within 48 hours recommended for mitigation credit under H-100 Section 6.2.

2.2.2 **Automobile (A-300):** notify within 7 days; hit-and-run within 24 hours for uninsured motorist property damage (A-300 Section 7.7).

2.3 **Information at first notice (FNOL).** Policy number, date of loss, location, brief description, contact details, whether police or fire attended, and whether anyone was injured.

2.4 **Claim number assignment.** Format CLM-YYYY-NNNNNN issued at FNOL; this number must appear on all subsequent correspondence.

2.5 **Cross-policy losses.** When home and auto are involved (e.g., garage fire with vehicle), a single **master claim** MC- prefix may link files; adjusters coordinate per Section 5.4.

## 3. Evidence and Documentation

3.1 **General list.**

3.1.1 Photographs or video of damage before permanent repairs.

3.1.2 Repair estimates from licensed vendors (fictional registry VR-12).

3.1.3 Receipts for emergency mitigation (tarps, dry-out, towing).

3.1.4 Police report when theft, vandalism, hit-and-run, or injury occurs.

3.1.5 For water claims, plumbing invoices and appliance serial numbers if sudden discharge is alleged (H-100 Section 5.2).

3.2 **Foundation and sudden water disputes.** Engineering report **ER-FND** within 15 days when invoking H-100 Section 5.3.3.

3.3 **Auto collision.** Photos of all vehicles, driver licences, insurance cards, witness statements, and dash-camera footage if available.

3.4 **Proof of ownership** for high-value personal property items over $5,000.

3.5 **Sworn proof of loss** form PPL-100 required before final payment on property claims exceeding $25,000 fictional threshold.

3.6 Insureds must not dispose of damaged property until authorized, except for health and safety mitigation documented in the claim file.

## 4. Investigation

4.1 **Adjuster assignment.** Within 2 business days of FNOL, a **file owner** is assigned: Desk Adjuster (DA), Field Property Adjuster (FPA), or Auto Damage Appraiser (ADA).

4.2 **Inspection.** On-site inspection scheduled within 5 business days for property; auto may use virtual photo estimate for minor damage under $3,500.

4.3 **Coverage review.** Adjuster verifies policy version, endorsements, deductibles, exclusions, and peril triggers (e.g., sudden vs gradual water per H-100 Section 5.3).

4.4 **Independent experts.** We may appoint hydrologists, engineers, biomechanics consultants, or fire investigators at our expense when coverage or causation is disputed.

4.5 **Interview.** Recorded statements may be taken from insureds, drivers, and witnesses.

4.6 **Fraud referral.** Files with inconsistent timelines may be referred to Special Investigations Unit SIU-DEMO; cooperation is required under policy conditions.

4.7 **Engineering hold.** Foundation infiltration claims are placed on engineering hold until ER-FND or soil report SR-22 is received when territory rules apply (H-100 Section 10.5).

## 5. Review and Decision

5.1 **Coverage determination letter (CDL).** Issued within 15 business days of complete FNOL unless catastrophe or engineering hold extends timeline.

5.2 **CDL contents.** Covered perils, applicable exclusions, deductibles, reserves, and next steps.

5.3 **Partial denial.** If part of a loss is excluded (e.g., gradual mould excluded but sudden pipe burst covered), CDL explains allocation methodology AL-METH-2.

5.4 **Multi-policy coordination.** Liability from auto may pay third-party bodily injury while property handles garage structure under H-100; reserves are not duplicated.

5.5 **Payment authority.**

5.5.1 Repairs: payment to shop via fictional direct repair network or to insured.

5.5.2 Total loss auto: ACV plus taxes minus deductible.

5.5.3 Additional living expense (if H-100 endorsement **H-ALE**): advance payment after 72 hours displacement.

5.6 **Closing.** File closed when payment issued and releases signed; reopened if supplemental damage reported within 12 months with new evidence.

## 6. Communication Standards

6.1 Status updates at least every 10 business days while investigation is open.

6.2 Language of service: English or French upon request in fictional bilingual regions.

6.3 Authorized representatives require written **AOR-1** form.

## 7. Appeals and Disputes

7.1 **Internal appeal.** If you disagree with coverage or amount, submit **Appeal Form AP-200** within 60 days of CDL with new evidence.

7.2 **Appeal review.** Senior adjuster (not original file owner) responds within 20 business days.

7.3 **Ombudsman.** Unresolved disputes may be escalated to ABC Assurances Ombudsman Office (fictional) per H-100 Section 10.3.

7.4 **Mediation and appraisal.**

7.4.1 Property repair amount disputes: appraisal per AP-PROP-4 (two appraisers + umpire).

7.4.2 Auto repair disputes: AP-AUTO-7 referenced in A-300 Section 9.4.

7.5 **Arbitration** only if fictional optional clause ARB-1 was purchased on Declarations.

7.6 Appeals do not extend statutory limitation periods in real law; here all deadlines are fictional for classroom use.

## 8. Appeals Evidence Checklist (Summary)

8.1 Contradictory expert reports with credentials.

8.2 Time-stamped photos showing progression of damage.

8.3 Weather data from fictional bureau W-API for sudden vs gradual arguments.

8.4 Complete contractor scope distinguishing rebuild vs cosmetic.

## 9. Special Claim Types

9.1 **Total loss home** with mortgage: payment may include fictional lender clause.

9.2 **Theft** with no forced entry: SIU review per H-100 theft conditions.

9.3 **Ride-share auto** claims require trip log matching **A-RSH** endorsement.

9.4 **Sewer backup** claims require proof of **H-WBU** and maintenance log for sump if applicable.

## 10. Records Retention and RAG Demo Notes

10.1 Claim files are retained 7 fictional years for training audits.

10.2 Documents indexed for RAG should include policy form, endorsements, FNOL transcript, photos metadata, CDL, and appeal outcome.

10.3 **Educational disclaimer.** Procedures mirror industry practice in outline only; numbers and forms are invented.

12.1 **FNOL** — first notice of loss.

12.2 **CDL** — coverage determination letter.

12.3 **Reserve** — estimated ultimate payment on file.

12.4 **SIU** — special investigations unit.

12.5 **Subrogation** — recovery pursuit against third parties.

## 13. Timetable Summary (Business Days, Fictional)

13.1 FNOL acknowledgment: same day automated, human within 1 day.

13.2 Adjuster assignment: 2 days.

13.3 Property inspection target: 5 days (excluding catastrophe).

13.4 CDL target: 15 days from complete FNOL.

13.5 Appeal response: 20 days from AP-200 receipt.

13.6 Catastrophe CP-9 may double inspection interval but requires public notice on portal.
